## Weekly Net

Runs on **Wednesday nights at 2000 hours** on the [N4UH Repeater **145.410**](https://www.repeaterbook.com/repeaters/details.php?state_id=37&ID=72) with Negative Offset and PL tone of **136.5**. Check-ins are encouraged.   
The alternate repeater is the [N4UH **443.250**](https://www.repeaterbook.com/repeaters/details.php?state_id=37&ID=330).
