# Rowan County Amateur Radio Emergency Service

![ARES Logo](/images/ares-logo.png)
![NC Flag](/images/ncflag.gif)
![RARS Logo](/images/rars-logo.jpg)

**Welcome to the home for Rowan County Amateur Radio Emergency Service**

ARES (`Amateur Radio Emergency Service`) is designed to assist with communications in times where normal modes of communications are no longer usable.

![When All Else Fails](/images/ares-waef.jpg)
